# Required human decisions

Apply the shared [execution-mode policy](execution-modes.md). Use a human wait
only when the decision needs knowledge, judgment or authority the model cannot
supply within its delegation.

## Record

Give each required decision a stable item/question identity. Record the focused
question and relevant evidence, with options and a recommendation where useful.
Explain why human input is needed and which work or milestone depends on the
answer. Use the owning role's existing immutable artifacts and wait state.

## Carry forward

Ask required questions directly, prioritizing those that block work or completion.
Preserve each unresolved
decision and its dependencies across roles, reports and resumes. Independent
authorized work may continue; dependent work cannot be called ready while its
required decision remains unresolved. Disclosure and an intermediate PR do not
resolve the decision.

Route required product or architectural choices discovered in later phases to
the existing planning or design wait, preserving prior work and evidence.

## Resolve

Resolve only the decisions settled by a relevant direct answer, new evidence or
applicable delegation. Retain the resolution and its provenance, distinguishing
human answers from agent decisions. Preserve earlier records and any questions
still unanswered; silence, unrelated replies and dismissed question cards do not
establish resolution. Reuse recorded answers rather than asking again.
